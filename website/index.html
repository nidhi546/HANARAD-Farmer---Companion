About the App

Built for Farmers,
by People Who Care

HANARAD Farmer-Companion was created to bridge the information gap faced by small and marginal farmers. We believe every farmer deserves access to the same quality of data and insights as large agricultural corporations — for free.

🛰️

NASA-Powered

Real historical and forecast weather data directly from NASA POWER satellites and Open-Meteo — the same data scientists use.

🌍

Truly Multilingual

Available in English, Hindi, Gujarati, and Filipino — more languages coming soon. Every farmer deserves tools in their own language.

📵

Works Offline

Core features work without internet. Cached weather data, saved farms, and crop diaries are always available on your device.

🔒

Privacy First

Your farm data stays on your device. We never sell personal data. Transparent privacy policy, clearly explained.

Features

Everything a Farmer Needs

🌦️

NASA Weather Intelligence

7-day forecasts, 10-day historical data, and climate profiles for any location. Know exactly what's coming before it hits your field.

  • ✓ Temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind
  • ✓ Extreme weather alerts
  • ✓ Historical climate comparison
  • ✓ Soil surface data from NASA
🌱

Smart Crop Advisor

Get crop recommendations tailored to your location's climate. Know which crops will thrive and the best time to sow.

🔬

Disease Scanner

Photograph your crop leaves. AI analyzes for diseases and gives treatment recommendations and medicine suggestions.

📊

Live Mandi Prices

Real-time market prices for cotton, wheat, groundnut, and more. Compare across markets to sell at the best price.

🗺️

Farm Map & GPS Walk

Draw your farm boundary on the map or walk the border with GPS tracking. Calculate area in acres, hectares, or bigha.

📅

Crop Calendar

Task-by-task schedule from sowing to harvest. Get reminders for fertilization, irrigation, and spraying at the right time.

💰

Expense Tracker

Track seeds, fertilizer, labour, and harvest income. See your net profit or loss per season at a glance.

🏛️

Govt. Schemes

Browse PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima, Kisan Credit Card, and other schemes. Know your eligibility and apply directly.

🧮

Smart Farm Calculators

Professional-grade calculators built for farmers:

  • ✓ Loan EMI calculator
  • ✓ Fertilizer quantity calculator
  • ✓ Seed quantity estimator
  • ✓ Land area unit converter
